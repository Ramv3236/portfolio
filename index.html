04 — About

Hi, I'm Rama Sai Mohith Varma Alluri.

I'm a video editor, graphic designer and AI filmmaker based in Hyderabad. Most of my recent work is for healthcare: doctor-focused educational reels and patient-friendly social posts that explain things like snoring, screen time, hearing tests and dental care in a way people actually stop to read.

Before design, I worked in fast-paced operations in London and at the Dubai World Trade Centre (including Formula 1 and royal events). That taught me to work calmly under pressure and always deliver on time.
